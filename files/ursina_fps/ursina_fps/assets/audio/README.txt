Place your sound files here (.wav, .ogg or .mp3). Names must match exactly:

  bgm.ogg          looping combat music
  gunshot.wav      weapon fire
  dry_fire.wav     empty-magazine click
  reload.wav       reload
  hit_marker.wav   bullet hits an enemy
  enemy_pain.wav   enemy damaged
  enemy_death.wav  enemy killed
  player_hurt.wav  player damaged

Anything missing falls back to a procedural ursfx beep (bgm falls back to silence).
